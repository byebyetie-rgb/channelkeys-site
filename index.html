The public pharma reference tables, already joined.

Channel Keys packages five U.S. federal datasets on one set of keys (NDC-11, application number, labeler, NPI) and refreshes them weekly with a row-level change log. Delivered natively on Snowflake Marketplace, no files to download.

Find us on Snowflake Marketplace

FDA NDC Directory

One row per 11-digit NDC with a crosswalk for every 10-digit format, marketing dates and discontinuation flags.

FDA Orange Book

Products, patents and exclusivity rolled up to one row per NDA/ANDA with an estimated loss-of-exclusivity date.

Medicaid SDUD

State drug utilization by NDC, state, year and quarter, typed and keyed to the same NDC-11.

340B OPAIS

Covered entities, contract pharmacies, ship-to sites, NPIs and Medicaid numbers, with active flags and change history.

CMS Open Payments

General payments summarized by manufacturer, by product with the NDC resolved, and by recipient.

Change log

Every refresh records adds, removals and field changes, so "what changed since last week" is one query.

Provenance

All source data is published by U.S. government agencies (FDA, CMS, HRSA). Each refresh ships a meta_source_run table with the URL, size, SHA-256 and server timestamp of every file used. Derived fields (normalizations, joins, estimated LOE dates, manufacturer matches) are ours and should be validated before regulatory or contractual use. Channel Keys is not affiliated with or endorsed by FDA, CMS or HRSA.
