Documentation

Tables and columns in the Channel Keys listing on Snowflake Marketplace. Refer to objects as CORE.<TABLE> under the database name you choose when you get the listing.

Keys and conventions

  • NDC11: 11-digit, zero-padded 5-4-2 NDC. XREF_NDC_FORMATS maps every FDA 10-digit format to it.
  • APPL_TYPE + APPL_NO: Orange Book application key (N = NDA, A = ANDA), APPL_NO zero-padded to 6 digits.
  • LABELER_CODE: first 5 digits of the NDC. DIM_LABELER carries the best labeler name and the matched Open Payments manufacturer.
  • ID_340B: HRSA covered entity ID. Contract pharmacies key on ID_340B + CONTRACT_ID.
  • SNAPSHOT_DATE: the refresh the row came from. CHANGE_LOG compares consecutive refreshes.
  • Derived fields (EST_LOE_DATE, IS_DISCONTINUED, IS_ACTIVE, manufacturer matches) are ours; validate before regulatory or contractual use.
