Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Channel Keys ("we", "us") publishes reference data products on Snowflake Marketplace and operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Website. This site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages with DNS and proxying by Cloudflare. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and we set no cookies. GitHub and Cloudflare may log standard server data (IP address, user agent, requested page) under their own privacy policies.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your message and address to reply and for our records.
  • Snowflake Marketplace. When you request or purchase a listing, Snowflake shares with us the business contact details and consumer account information needed to fulfill the request and to provide support, under the Snowflake Marketplace terms. We use that information only to deliver and support the product and to send service notices about it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use consumer information for advertising.
  • Our data products are built from public datasets published by U.S. government agencies and contain no protected health information. Where a public dataset includes information about individuals (for example, CMS Open Payments covered recipients or 340B authorizing officials), we republish only the fields the agency publishes.

Data retention and security

Correspondence and Marketplace consumer records are kept for as long as you are a consumer of our listings and for a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and support. Credentials and infrastructure are protected with key-based authentication and least-privilege roles.

Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@channelkeys.net. We respond within 30 days.

Third parties

Snowflake Marketplace: snowflake.com/privacy-policy. GitHub Pages: GitHub privacy statement. Cloudflare: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
