Support

Email support@channelkeys.net. We answer within one business day; data-quality reports usually within hours.

Refresh schedule

The listing refreshes every Sunday at 03:40 US Eastern. META_REFRESH shows when the current data was loaded; META_SOURCE_RUN shows the URL, size, SHA-256 and agency timestamp of every source file behind it. The refresh ledger on the home page shows the same information.

Reporting a data issue

Send the table name, the key (an NDC-11, application number or 340B ID), what you expected and what you saw. If the agency file is wrong we say so and point to it; if our transformation is wrong we fix it in the next refresh and note it in the listing's release notes.

Requests

Need another public source joined in, a different grain, or a private listing with custom tables? Write to hello@channelkeys.net.

Getting the listing: open Snowflake Marketplace in your account, search for Channel Keys, and choose Get. The database appears in your account under the name you choose; refer to tables as CORE.<TABLE>.
